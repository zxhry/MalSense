Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ LEGAL_UPDATED }} · Terms of Use

In short

  • Your APK is deleted right after the scan. MalSense never installs or runs it. Your original file stays on your device.
  • VirusTotal only receives the file's fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) – never the file itself.
  • We keep only what the website needs: your name, email, a scrambled (hashed) password, and the results of your scans.
  • No advertising, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

MalSense is a final-year project at Universiti Sains Islam Malaysia (USIM): an explainable AI-powered Android malware scanner and security-awareness platform for educational use. This policy explains what personal data MalSense collects and why. MalSense is an educational project, and this policy is written following the principles of Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), as amended in 2024: telling you what is collected and why, keeping it secure, and giving you access to it, correction of it, and the right to withdraw your consent.

2. What we collect

Account detailsYour name and email address. Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash – nobody, including the administrator, can read it.
Google sign-inIf you use “Continue with Google”, Google shares only your name and email address. MalSense never sees your Google password.
Scan resultsFor each scan: file name, file size, hashes (SHA-256, SHA-1, MD5), app name and package, minimum Android version, the permissions it requests, the AI verdict and explanation, the VirusTotal result, and the date. Guest scans are saved without any account.
Reported APKsIf you report an APK, the file is kept in a locked (quarantined) folder together with your reason, optional message and optional contact email.
Account activityWhen you registered and last logged in.
CookiesOne session cookie to keep you logged in (a longer-lasting one only if you tick “Remember me”), and a browser setting that remembers your light/dark theme choice. No advertising or analytics cookies.

3. What happens to an uploaded APK

  • MalSense saves a temporary copy under a random name, reads it (static analysis only – it is never installed or run), and deletes the copy as soon as the scan finishes.
  • Only reported APKs are kept, so the administrator can check them and, if suitable, add them to the training data that keeps the AI model up to date.

4. Why we use your data

  • To scan your file and show the result, explanation and advice.
  • To keep your scan history and let you download reports (registered users).
  • To verify your email address and help you reset a forgotten password.
  • To keep the service secure, and to improve the AI model with reported APKs.
  • To produce anonymous statistics and threat-intelligence indicators (file hashes of detected malware) – these do not include your name or email.

5. Services we share data with

VirusTotalOnly the file's SHA-256 hash, to check it against antivirus engines. The file is never uploaded.
Google Gemini (AI quiz)The app's name, its AI verdict, and the names of the permissions it requests, to write quiz questions. No personal data is sent.
Google sign-inOnly if you choose it – Google confirms who you are and shares your name and email.
Gmail (email)To send your verification and password-reset emails.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, and there are no advertisers.

6. How we protect it

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt; email links that expire; protection against forged forms (CSRF).
  • Users can only see their own scans; only the administrator can see all scan logs.
  • Uploaded files are stored under random names and deleted after scanning; reported files are quarantined.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and scan history are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • If an account is deleted, its scan results are kept only as anonymous records (no longer linked to you).
  • Reported APKs are kept while they are useful for checking and training the model.

8. Your rights

In line with the PDPA, you may ask to see or correct your personal data, or withdraw your consent (which closes your account). You may also ask us to delete your account and personal data, and we will do so on request. You can always use MalSense as a guest without giving any personal data. To make a request, use the Feedback form.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your data (to see, correct or delete it): please use the Feedback form and choose “My data / privacy request”. We reply to the email address you give there.

We may update this policy as MalSense changes; the date at the top shows the latest version.
